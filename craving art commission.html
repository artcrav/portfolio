Craving ART Accepts

What We Draw

Original Characters (OC) · Fanart (personal use only) · Chibi, Flower & Food art · Couple art (any gender) · Simple animals & pets · Simple backgrounds · Clothing changes & recolors · Cute, soft & aesthetic themes

Craving ART Don't Accept

What we won't draw

NSFW / R18 · Mecha (full robot detail) · Gore & extreme violence · Complex armor · Realistic portraits · Hate, offensive, or harmful content · Homework (do it yourself) · Strictly protected IPs (Disney, Sanrio, Nintendo, Marvel/DC, etc.)
